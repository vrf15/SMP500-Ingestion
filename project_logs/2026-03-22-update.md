\## More on EC2 being overwhelmed



&#x09;Diagnosing the EC2 issues was hard. It's my first time getting very hands-on with a full-stack project. I discovered that Airflow parses its DAG every minute by default. That's not an issue when you have a strong instance/system to run on or if you have a small amount of DAGs, but having all the new DAGs plus a small t3a.large instance was not ideal for the setup I had. Docker also created containers I didn't need, and my previous SmartVault project consistently pulled packages SP500 from the .env config.

&#x09;

&#x09;To combat these issues, I delayed the scheduled parses to every 5 minutes (300s) instead. I also created a Docker image and removed the trigerrer container.
